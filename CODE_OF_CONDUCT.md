# Code of Conduct

Samrian contributors must keep project spaces constructive, technical, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat people with respect across differences in experience and background.
- Critique code and decisions, not individuals.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction without hostility.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or confidential information.
- Keep outreach discussions lawful, consent-aware, and relevant to legitimate product use.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or sustained personal attacks.
- Doxxing, impersonation, spam, or deliberate disruption.
- Encouraging fraud, phishing, credential theft, or abusive email campaigns.
- Retaliation against good-faith reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when behavior
harms the project or community. Report conduct privately through repository maintainer contact
channels. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

Maintainers should document decisions, protect reporter privacy, avoid conflicts of interest, and
apply proportionate consequences consistently.
